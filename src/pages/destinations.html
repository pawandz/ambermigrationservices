CA
North America

Canada

Canada offers structured pathways for international students, skilled workers, visitors and families, with a strong emphasis on clear eligibility criteria.

Pathways we guide clients on

StudyStudy permit applications for designated learning institutions.
WorkWork permit applications, including employer-specific permits where eligible.
VisitorVisitor visa (TRV) and eTA guidance for tourism and family visits.
Permanent ResidencyProfile assessment for Express Entry and Provincial Nominee programs.
FamilySpouse, partner and dependent child sponsorship applications.

Official information: Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada

Australia

Australia combines world-class universities with a points-tested skilled migration system and a range of family and visitor options.

Pathways we guide clients on

StudyStudent visa applications, including Genuine Student requirements.
SkilledSkills assessment guidance and skilled visa eligibility reviews.
VisitorVisitor visa applications for tourism and visiting family.
PartnerPartner visa applications and relationship evidence preparation.

Official information: Department of Home Affairs

United Kingdom

The UK's points-based system covers students and skilled workers, alongside visitor and family routes with detailed evidence requirements.

Pathways we guide clients on

StudentStudent visa applications with a Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies.
Skilled WorkerSkilled Worker visa applications with a licensed sponsor.
VisitorStandard Visitor visa applications for tourism, family and business visits.
FamilySpouse and partner visa applications.

Official information: GOV.UK – Visas and immigration
