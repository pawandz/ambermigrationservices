  1. 01

    Initial Consultation

    A focused conversation to understand your goals, background, timelines and any concerns. We explain how the process works and give an initial view of the options that may suit you.

    What to prepare

    • Your goals and preferred destination(s)
    • Education and work history
    • Any previous visa applications or refusals
  2. 02

    Profile Assessment

    We review your eligibility in detail against the relevant criteria, identify suitable pathways and highlight any gaps. You receive a clear recommendation and a written scope and fee for the next steps.

    You'll receive

    • Recommended pathway(s) and reasoning
    • Personalised document checklist
    • Written service scope and fees
  3. 03

    Application Preparation

    We help you gather, review and organise your documents, complete forms accurately and prepare a consistent, well-presented application for submission.

    Our role

    • Document review for completeness and consistency
    • Form completion guidance
    • Final file check before submission
  4. 04

    Application Follow-up

    After submission, we keep you informed throughout the agreed service, help you respond to requests for further information, and explain next steps once a decision is made.

    Ongoing support

    • Status guidance and updates
    • Help with additional document requests
    • Pre-departure next steps after a decision
Setting expectations

What we can, and can't, promise.

Responsible advice means being clear about where our role ends and the authority's decision begins.

We will

  • Give honest, personalised advice
  • Confirm fees and scope in writing
  • Prepare your application carefully
  • Keep you informed

We can't

  • Guarantee a visa approval
  • Promise processing times
  • Influence an authority's decision
  • Submit false or misleading information
