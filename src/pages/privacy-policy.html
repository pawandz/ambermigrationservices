1. Introduction

Amber Migration Services ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through this website and in the course of providing our immigration consultancy services.

2. Information we collect

  • Enquiry details you provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, service of interest, preferred destination and message.
  • Client information you share during an engagement, which may include identity, education, employment, financial and family information required for an application.
  • Technical information such as browser type and pages visited, if analytics tools are enabled on this website.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and arrange consultations.
  • To provide the services you request, including assessing eligibility and preparing applications.
  • To communicate with you about your enquiry or application.
  • To meet legal, regulatory and record-keeping obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Sharing information

We share information only where needed to provide our services or where required by law. For example, with immigration authorities when you instruct us to submit an application, or with service providers who help us operate (such as email or form-handling services) under appropriate confidentiality obligations.

5. Storage & security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss or misuse. We keep information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described above or as required by law.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

7. Cookies & third parties
